04 / A LITTLE WINDOW. A LOVELY RITUAL.

Ring the bell.
Meet your glass.

La buchetta del vino

In Florence, little windows in palace walls became a way to sell wine straight from the cellar to the street. A small opening with a wonderfully sociable story.

We’re bringing that spirit to Soi 33/1. Stand out front, ring the bell, and we’ll pass a glass through our very own wine window. A little Florence, right here in Bangkok.
