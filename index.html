A FLORENTINE TRADITION · A BANGKOK HELLO

La buchetta
del vino.

A little wine window.
A lovely way to begin an evening.

In Renaissance Florence, wine-growing families passed flasks through small openings in their palace walls, selling directly to people on the street. These buchette del vino became part of the city’s everyday life.

A little more history +

During Florence’s plague of the 1630s, wine sellers also used the windows to limit direct contact, collecting coins on a metal paddle. Centuries later, some of the city’s historic windows are serving again.

Our little window takes its inspiration from this Florentine tradition, with a bell, a glass, and a warm Bangkok welcome.
