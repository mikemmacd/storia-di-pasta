SMALL PLACE. PLENTY OF PERSONALITY.

Four little corners.
One good evening.

Just 50 square metres, with room for a few new friends.
Come for pasta, stay for the music, the laughter, and one more conversation.

01 / THE COURTYARD

Il Cortile

A little Tuscan alley in the middle of Bangkok. Pull up a seat, take in the room, and let the conversation find you.

A little escape, close to home.
02 / THE DINING ROOM

La Sala

Close tables. Familiar faces. The easy pleasure of catching up over a bowl of pasta, with nowhere else you need to be.

Make yourself at home.
03 / THE COSY CORNER

La Cantina

Settle in a little closer. A corner for long chats, shared stories, and evenings that don’t need much of a plan.

Stay a little longer.
04 / THE FRONT STEP

Il Patio

Where the evening spills onto the soi. Meet out front, ring the bell, and we’ll pass a glass through the wine window.

A little Florentine ritual, with a Bangkok hello.

Ring. Say ciao. Cin cin.
